PlayScout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-22

Publisher: Nicholas Alan Vandervelden

Information and permissions

PlayScout stores no user accounts, location history, or analytics. The detailed topographic and aerial map layers request public USGS map tiles using coordinates for the visible map area and normal network metadata, including the requesting IP address, browser/operating system, and request time. The map does not send the raw GPS coordinate, saved parks, private notes, or typed park searches to USGS. If you use Near Me while online detail is active, the tile requests can reveal an approximate area around the device. Built-in roads work offline without map requests. No ad or cross-app tracking is used.

Local saved places and notes

Your saved playgrounds, comparison lists, and private notes are stored exclusively in local device storage using standard iOS storage facilities. They are never synchronized to external servers or accessible to third parties. You can export your saved places via native iOS sharing or delete them at any time directly within the app.

Services and external links

Online topographic tiles are requested directly from USGS The National Map; aerial map tiles are requested from USGS/USDA NAIP services. USGS says its system logs record IP address, browser/OS, access time, and requested pages/information for internal site management; the published notice does not specify a retention period. See https://www.usgs.gov/office-of-the-director/privacy-policies. Other park-source and directions links open outside PlayScout in Safari or Maps. PlayScout runs no map proxy or user-data server.

Retention and choices

PlayScout does not receive or retain the online map tile requests. USGS processes its service logs under the USGS privacy notice; the notice does not state a specific retention period. Saved parks, comparison lists, and notes remain on the device and are deleted when the app is removed.
